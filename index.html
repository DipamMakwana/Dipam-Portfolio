03Founder project

Growth that travels both ways.

Veparo serves small businesses with growth marketing, social media management, and website design, and separately connects creators with brand sponsorships. The name comes from the Gujarati word for business, "vepar," with a modern "-o" suffix. The insight that makes it work: today's small-business client can become tomorrow's sponsor for a creator, and vice versa, so every client on one side is a potential lead on the other.

Node.js / Express SQLite JWT Auth Stripe Checkout Role-Based Dashboards
Visit Veparo ↗
The model

Two client bases, one referral loop. A small business that hires Veparo for growth marketing is also a candidate to sponsor a creator in Veparo's network, and a creator being managed for sponsorships is also a prospective small-business client down the line. Retention on one side compounds the pipeline on the other.

What I built
  • Single-file animated marketing site with a two-stage load sequence and four expandable service cards with live pricing.
  • Stripe checkout wired to the website-design pricing tier, plus success/cancel redirect flows.
  • Node.js/Express + SQLite backend with JWT-authenticated accounts, split by business-owner and creator roles.
Where BA instincts showed up
  • Modeled the two-sided flywheel before writing a line of code: same requirements-first habit as scoping a BRD.
  • Role-based dashboard tracks sponsorship deals for creator accounts, the one number that actually matters to that user.
  • Debugged real setup friction end to end (environment config, native module compilation, local API verification) rather than handing it off.
